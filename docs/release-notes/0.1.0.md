First release.

- Sunburst chart of any volume or folder, drawn while the scan runs.
- Scan several volumes at once, each in its own tab.
- Show in Finder, Quick Look, Get Info, Open, copy path, Move to Trash.
- Collector: gather items across scans and remove them in one go.
- Hidden Space: what the volume counts as used that the scan cannot see —
  purgeable caches, local Time Machine snapshots, unreadable trees.
- Scan as Administrator through a privileged helper, for other users' files.
- Saved scans reopen instantly.
